Gene Crossing Table

Pure — same gene twice (homozygous) Co-expressed — both genes active Partial — codominant unlocks the first line of a recessive’s bonus Masked — dominant hides recessive (carrier) Latent — two different recessives, both dormant

Rarity Weights

Each allele rolls a rarity first, then a gene of that rarity. Higher weight = more common.

Factor Editor

Written for D&D 5e. Edit any box freely, e.g. “+1 Strength, resistance to fire damage”. Bonus applies when the gene is expressed; Pure bonus applies when the offspring has two copies of it.

Special Combinations

Extra bonuses for a specific pair of genes (either order), e.g. Human × Demon → Hybrid.
