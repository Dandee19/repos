How Inheritance Works

Pure — the same gene twice: its full bonus plus its pure bonus Co-expressed — two dominant/codominant genes: both give their full bonus Awakened — a codominant gene fully awakens a recessive one: both full Suppressed — a dominant gene is full; the recessive gives only its first line Hidden Pair — two different recessives: each gives only its first line

Demons are made by the Demon King's blood, not born, so no demon genes can be inherited. A gene like Demon Digestion is a human trait.
